Route · Start — 5 m

Your first breath-hold, done calmly

One instructor in the water with you, one skill at a time: relaxation, breathing, equalisation and safe, supervised depth in the pool.

Price
Ask for the current price — confirmed when you book a date.
Duration
Theory + pool sessions; exact schedule confirmed with your date.
Equipment & place
Pool in Tallinn; equipment arranged with the course.

Price, schedule and prerequisites are confirmed by the team before you commit — nothing is charged online.

Who this is for

  • You are new to freediving or have only snorkelled.
  • You are comfortable in water and can swim.
  • You want instruction in English (Estonian also available).

Who should ask us first

  • You are under 18 — age rules are confirmed when you ask.
  • You have a medical condition that affects breath-hold or water sports.
  • You want a short taster rather than a full course — tell us, we will point you right.

What you experience and learn

You leave with a working calm-water routine — not a list of topics. You learn to slow your breathing before a dive, to equalise early and gently, to trust a buddy system, and to end every dive with a clean recovery. The goal of the first course is not depth; it is that the water starts to feel like a place you can think in.

Introduction sessions and the full beginner course are different things — if you are unsure which you want, say so in the form and the team will explain both before you choose.

    Route · Rummu — 12 m

    A guided fundive in a flooded quarry

    Rummu is a former prison work site that was flooded and left as it was. Diving it is worth doing properly: guided, with surface safety, and only when conditions allow.

    A fundive is not a beginner course. It is for people who already have freediving training. Weather, water temperature, visibility and access change through the year — the final go/no-go is confirmed by the guide based on conditions.

    Photo slot: a real Rummu gallery is on the missing-inputs list — current photos are from open-water training.

    What we ask of you

    • Freediving certification or other proof of training.
    • When you last dived, and the depth you are comfortable at.
    • Your own equipment status — what you have, what you need.

    What is conditional

    • Date and site access — weather and season decide.
    • Visibility and temperature — never promised, always briefed.
    • Cancellation and transfer terms — confirmed with your booking, in writing.

    How the dive is run

    • Buddy principle and surface safety on every dive.
    • Briefing on site: entry, lines, signals, limits.
    • Small groups; the guide dives with you.

    Weather-dependent · go/no-go by the guide · no one-click booking

    We deliberately do not sell instant Rummu bookings: suitability, risks, conditions and cancellation are agreed person-to-person first. The story of the place itself — depths, seasons, what it is like — lives on freedive.ee, the Estonia destination guide.

      Route · Plan Ahead ↑ surface

      Planning happens on dry land

      Estonia rewards planning: the flooded quarry at Rummu, Baltic open water and a real winter with ice make different months genuinely different trips.

      Why plan Estonia

      A flooded prison work site with structures still underwater, cold clear-water seasons, and under-ice diving in winter for those trained for it. The full destination story, month by month, is on freedive.ee.

      A realistic shape for 2–4 days

      Arrival and pool session in Tallinn; open-water or Rummu day when conditions allow; a flexible reserve day. We build the actual plan with you — there is no fixed package or package price.

      What to tell us

      Where you are travelling from, your window, how many days and people, your training level, and what you most want from the trip — Rummu, open water, pool work or photography.

        FAQ ↑ surface

        Questions people actually ask

        Do I need any experience to start freediving in Estonia?

        No prior freediving experience is needed for the Start route — it is built for complete beginners. You should be comfortable in the water and able to swim; exact requirements are confirmed when you ask for a start date.

        Is the training available in English?

        Yes, courses and guided dives are led in English as well as Estonian. Tell us your preferred language in the form and the session is planned accordingly.

        Can I book a Rummu fundive as my first freediving experience?

        No — a Rummu fundive is not a beginner course. It is a guided dive for people who already have freediving training; if you are new, begin with the Start route and Rummu can be your next step.

        Is a Rummu dive guaranteed once I book it?

        No dive is guaranteed: weather, water temperature, visibility and access at Rummu change, and the final go/no-go decision is made by the guide based on conditions. That decision process is part of what you are booking.

        How far ahead should I plan a freediving trip to Estonia?

        Six to twelve months ahead is a comfortable window for planning an Estonia freediving trip. That leaves room to match the season you want — summer open water or winter ice — with dates, group size and logistics.
