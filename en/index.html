Freediving — 6 m

Calm, disciplined, safety-first

Freediving here is not an extreme sport — it is training in calm and attention. Every session starts with breathing and relaxation; depth comes from technique, not from pushing. Courses are taught in English and your instructor is in the water with you.

We train in Tallinn, in the pool and in open water, year-round — in winter under the ice, for those who are ready and guided.

See the freediving routes

Estonia / Rummu

A flooded quarry, left as it was

Rummu is a guided and conditional experience for trained freedivers: weather, water temperature, visibility and access change, and the final call is made by the guide on site. We do not promise visibility, depth or weather — we promise guiding and a safety process.

Weather-dependent · go/no-go by the guide

Safety & Team — 14 m

Instructors who dive with you

Safety is not an add-on here — it is the structure of every session: buddy principle, surface watch, clear hand signals and an instructor who is in the water, not on the shore. The qualifications below are the ones the team publishes itself.
